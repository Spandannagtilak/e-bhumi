## Brand & Style
The design system operates at the nexus of state sovereign authority, legal rigor, and high-density geographic engineering. Built for high-stakes governance, land administration authorities, and infrastructure conglomerates, the interface projects unquestionable institutional trust, absolute precision, and modern technological capability.

The visual style blends **Modern Enterprise Systematic** with precision **Technical HUD/Geospatial ergonomics**. It replaces bureaucratic heaviness with crisp optical hierarchies, zero-ambiguity state indications, and hyper-legible data dense displays. The aesthetic values structural reliability: razor-sharp boundaries, balanced atmospheric framing, and dedicated functional zones that transition seamlessly between legal-administrative document processing and complex GIS multi-polygon cartography.

## Colors
The color architecture relies on a deliberate balance of authoritative civic tones and purpose-driven semantic indicators.

- **Primary Brand & Structure (Deep Gov Slate / Navy)**: `#0F172A` defines navigation, structural headers, high-level frames, and primary text, grounding the portal in institutional stability. Sub-panels and peripheral sidebars utilize `#1E293B`.
- **Secondary (Active/Acquired Emerald)**: `#059669` (with accent `#10B981`) serves as the affirmative semantic color denoting completed acquisition, clear titles, vetted cadastral parcels, and verified land records.
- **Tertiary (GIS & Telemetry Sky)**: `#0284C7` (accent `#38BDF8`) is dedicated to spatial mapping, geospatial layer overlays, active polygon vertices, projection coordinates, and GIS HUD utilities.
- **Status Amber (Pending / Encumbrance / In-Progress)**: `#D97706` (with soft tint `#FEF3C7` and border `#F59E0B`) marks litigations, pending clearances, rehabilitation workflow states, and review checkpoints.
- **Error / Critical Alert (Disputed / Encroached)**: `#DC2626` flags boundary clashes, legal stay orders, and invalid survey numbers.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces**: Background canvases use crisp Indian Ash White (`#F8FAFC`), while primary surface cards and analytical sheets rest on `#FFFFFF`. Secondary borders and structural dividers use balanced neutral lines (`#E2E8F0` and `#CBD5E1`).

## Typography
Typography is tuned for intensive cross-referencing, multi-tier survey codes, geospatial telemetry, and statutory reports. 

- **Display & Section Titles (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Used for dashboard headers, portal identity, zone categorization, and modal headers. Provides clean structural modernism without excessive stylistic eccentricity.
- **Data, Body, & Legal Records (Inter)**: Built for high scanability in dense tabular grids, polygon metadata panels, and legal gazette records. Monospaced numbers (`font-feature-settings: 'tnum' on, 'zero' on`) must be enabled universally across numeric tables, survey coordinates, parcel IDs, and compensation amounts.
- **Labels & GIS Overlays (Inter Uppercase/Condensed tracking)**: Labels use higher letter tracking (`0.02em` to `0.05em`) and medium/semibold weights to ensure fast recognition when layered directly over topological maps or satellite orthophotos.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout system enforces a compact 8pt base grid with an inner 4pt baseline subdivision, optimized for dense administrative panels and dual-screen cadastral workstations.

### Structural Framework
- **Primary Grid**: A 12-column adaptive fluid grid with standard 16px (`1rem`) gutters on desktop, collapsible to 6 columns on tablet (breakpoint: 768px - 1023px) and single column on mobile (< 768px).
- **Workspace Partitioning**:
  - *Standard Dual-Pane Mode*: 380px fixed analytical sidebar on the left with fluid canvas on the right for GIS viewport or tabular record inspection.
  - *Full Survey Mode*: Collapsible sidebar reduced to an 64px icon rail, dedicating 100% remaining width to parcel boundary digitization.
- **Safe Dynamic Padding**: High-density table cells employ compact vertical padding (`6px` to `8px`) with horizontal padding (`12px`) to maximize above-the-fold record audits without visual crowding.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth hierarchy emphasizes crisp delineation and physical map layering over decorative floatation.

1. **Base Ground (Level 0)**: `#F8FAFC` for page canvases and rasterized cartographic tiles.
2. **Structural Surfaces (Level 1)**: `#FFFFFF` for primary cards, side navigation, data tables, and modal shells, delineated by clean ghost outlines (`1px solid #E2E8F0`). No blurry ambient shadows are permitted at this level.
3. **Floating HUD & Map Controls (Level 2)**: Geospatial telemetry chips, layer toggles, and coordinate status pins utilize sharp, micro-diffused functional shadows tinted with gov-navy: `0 2px 4px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`, framed by a razor-thin border (`1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`).
4. **Interactive Drawers & Flyouts (Level 3)**: Parcel ownership history panels, compensation calculators, and statutory modals use a structured elevation: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`, backed by a 20% slate overlay (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.4)`) with minimal backdrop-blur (`2px`).

## Shapes
The system strictly enforces a **Soft Structural (Level 1)** geometry. 

- Form inputs, tabular container boundaries, operational buttons, and data cards adhere to `0.25rem` (4px) radii.
- Secondary structural containers, floating GIS control heads, and dialog modals scale to `0.5rem` (8px).
- Pills and circular shapes are strictly reserved for unalterable metadata tags, status pills, and spatial node handles to maintain a stark semantic distinction between actionable cards and status tags.

## Components

### Buttons & Operational Actions
- **Primary**: Solid Deep Gov Slate (`#0F172A`) with high-contrast pure white text, 4px border-radius, 8px 16px padding. Focused states display a 2px offset ring in Sky Blue (`#0284C7`).
- **Secondary / Clearance Actions**: Vibrant Emerald (`#059669`) background for "Approve Award", "Verify Cadastral Boundary", or "Issue Gazette Notice".
- **Tertiary / Outlined**: Transparent background with `#CBD5E1` border, `#1E293B` text, and `#F1F5F9` hover state.

### Status Chips & Badges
- Statuses must pair a high-contrast text color with a soft tint background and a matching 1px border.
- **Acquired / Clear Title**: Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, border `#A7F3D0`. Prefixed with a solid 6px emerald status dot.
- **Pending / In Arbitration**: Background `#FFFBEB`, text `#92400E`, border `#FDE68A`.
- **Litigated / Encroached**: Background `#FEF2F2`, text `#991B1B`, border `#FECACA`.
- **Surveyed / GIS Verified**: Background `#F0F9FF`, text `#0369A1`, border `#BAE6FD`.

### Tabular Grids (Cadastral & Land Records)
- Sticky headers in `#F8FAFC` with bolded `11px` uppercase labels (`#475569`), framed by a bottom 2px border in `#CBD5E1`.
- Row height fixed at 40px for standard density. Alternating row background (`#FFFFFF` to `#FBFCFD`) with an active row hover highlight in `#F0F9FF` (`1px solid #0284C7` on the left border).
- Numeric alignment: Tabular figures right-aligned; survey token identifiers and Khasra/Gata numbers centered in monospaced badge styling.

### Input Fields & Search Queries
- Crisp 1px `#CBD5E1` border on `#FFFFFF` base. Focused inputs use `#0284C7` border and a subtle `#E0F2FE` focus-glow ring.
- Integrated coordinate inputs feature inline coordinate format selectors (DD, DMS, UTM) embedded within the input frame.

### GIS HUD & Map Overlay Controls
- Floating vertical toolbars anchored to top-right/bottom-left of map surfaces.
- White background (`#FFFFFF`) with 1px border (`#E2E8F0`), housing 32x32px icon buttons for layer toggles, polygon measurement, buffer zoning, and snap-to-vertex toggles.
- Bottom coordinates readout bar uses a compact slate translucent style (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.88)` with `#FFFFFF` text), displaying real-time WGS84/EPSG datum parameters and scale metrics.
